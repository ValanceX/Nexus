# Changelog

Every release of NEXUS, newest first, in the [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) layout. The project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org); until 1.0, a minor version may include breaking changes, always named in its notes.

Each entry links to the version's full release notes: what changed, how to upgrade, and what was verified.
